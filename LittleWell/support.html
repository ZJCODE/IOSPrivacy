Frequently Asked Questions / 常见问题

Q: Is my health data stored on your servers? / 问：我的健康数据会存储在您的服务器上吗？
A: No. Your weight, sleep, activity, and health data are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store this data on our servers.
答：不会。您的体重、睡眠、活动和健康数据均保存在您的设备本地，我们不会将其收集或存储到服务器。

Q: Does LittleWell integrate with Apple HealthKit? / 问：LittleWell 是否与 Apple HealthKit 集成？
A: Yes, if you choose to enable it. HealthKit access is optional and controlled entirely through your device settings. You can enable or disable it at any time.
答：是的，但仅在您选择启用的情况下。HealthKit 访问是可选的，完全由您在设备设置中控制，您可以随时开启或关闭。

Q: How do I back up my data? / 问：如何备份我的数据？
A: LittleWell stores data on your device. You can back up your device through iCloud or iTunes to keep your data safe.
答：LittleWell 将数据保存在设备本地。您可以通过 iCloud 或 iTunes 备份设备，以确保数据安全。

Q: Is the app free? / 问：这款应用是免费的吗？
A: Please check the App Store page for current pricing, features, and any available subscriptions or one-time purchases.
答：请在 App Store 页面查看当前定价、功能以及可用的订阅或一次性购买项目。

Q: I found a bug. How do I report it? / 问：我发现了一个 Bug，如何反馈？
A: Please email zhangjun310@live.com with a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and your device/iOS version.
答：请发送邮件至 zhangjun310@live.com，描述问题、复现步骤以及您的设备和 iOS 版本。
